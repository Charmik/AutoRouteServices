Cyprus: every road, paved or unpaved

The Esri SUSformer model (round 2, trained on the US, Provence, Bavaria, Victoria and Hokkaido; never on Cyprus) run on every OSM way in Cyprus it covers: 147,463 ways. Untagged ways have no surface tag, so the colour is the model’s guess. Tagged ways have a paved/unpaved surface tag the model never saw, so you can see where it disagrees with OSM. Click a road, judge it from the crop and the links.

Score. p(paved) from the Esri model with 4-flip averaging; ≥ 0.5 counts as paved. "vs OSM" compares the prediction with the OSM surface tag (tagged ways only). Ways with a surface value that is neither clearly paved nor unpaved are listed as untagged.

The crop is the 30 × 30 m Esri patch the model saw at the middle of the way, rotated so the road runs up. The model also saw up to 3 more patches along longer ways. Image date and provider come from Esri's World Imagery metadata; zoom 18 means about 0.45 m pixels instead of 0.3 m.

Keys. With a road selected: P paved, U unpaved, X can't tell, N next road in the list. Data: OSM extracts of Sept 2026; Esri World Imagery fetched Nov 2026. Ways: the model’s highway classes, at least 20 m long, no private access, no parking aisles or driveways. Zoom in to see ways as lines; zoomed out they are dots.
